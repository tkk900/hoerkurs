Hörkurs Deutsch

Learn German by listening, A1 to C2

    Each lesson teaches its new words by ear: the word, its meaning, the example sentence word by word, then the whole sentence, with pauses for you to repeat. A short spoken review ends every lesson. The screen stays on while you listen. With the German voice installed on your phone (see Settings), lessons keep playing without internet once this page is open.

    Settings

    Google's German voice, offline (Android)
    1. Open the phone's Settings and search for Text-to-speech output.
    2. Set Preferred engine to Speech Services by Google.
    3. Tap the gear next to it, then Install voice data, then Deutsch (Deutschland), and download it. Do the same for English.
    4. Come back to this page, pick the voice above and tap Test. Voices marked "on this device" work without internet.
